# Scouting Social Trends

Find what is spreading on short-video platforms right now and decide which pieces can become **a mechanic, a theme, or a video format** for a simple game.

**REQUIRED BACKGROUND:** `using-game-trend-skills`.

## Protocol

1. **Collect.** Search the current week and month with dates in the query. Cover: trending hashtags and sounds (TikTok Creative Center by country), weekly trend roundups, AI-filter and template trends, meme timelines (Know Your Meme), gaming-adjacent hashtags (#roblox, #minecraft, #satisfying, #asmr…), and the user's home country.
2. **Extract the verb.** Strip each trend down to its *action* and its *payoff*: cut, pop, peel, sort, stack, eat, clean, reveal, transform, collect, steal, merge. A trend with no verb can only be a **theme** or **video format**, not a mechanic.
3. **Run the five-gate filter.**

   | Gate | Pass when |
   |---|---|
   | **Muted 3-second read** | A muted clip makes the action and payoff obvious within 3 seconds |
   | **Verb fits a known loop** | The verb maps onto a proven casual loop (sort, merge, clear, draw, tap-timing…) |
   | **IP-safe** | No third-party characters, songs, brands or creator likenesses. Original characters only. |
   | **Shelf life ≥ build time** | The expected trend life is longer than the time to ship (or the trend becomes a reskin of an existing game) |
   | **Shareable output** | Play produces something worth posting: a result card, a timelapse, a before/after |

4. **Classify each survivor:**
   - **Mechanic:** a new game or new mode (needs ≥ 4 gates)
   - **Theme / reskin:** new skin or seasonal pack on an existing loop (fast, low risk)
   - **Video format:** a hook structure for organic posts and ads (for example, "proof-first": show the finished result, then the process)
5. **Estimate the window.** Use the trend's start date and its growth (rising / steady / fading). Seasonal trends have fixed ends, so plan backwards from the date.
6. **Write the output table** below. Put it in the current insight report, or save it as `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-social-trends.md`.

## Output

| Trend | Source (date) | Verb → payoff | Gates passed (n/5) | Use as | Window | Concrete game hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Finish with **the single best bet** and why.

## Meme rule

Meme waves (absurd AI characters, catchphrases) turn into games fast, but their windows are short and the characters often belong to someone else. **Make your own meme-able characters** in the trend's style. Then you own the IP, and every new character is a new video.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Trend X is huge, make a game about X" | Huge ≠ gamifiable. Find the verb and run the gates. |
| Using a viral song or character in the game | IP risk means store takedowns. Use the *structure*, not the asset. |
| Starting a 2-month build for a 3-week trend | Make it a reskin or a video format instead. |
| Global trends only | Also scan the home country. Local trends are cheap test beds for organic growth. |

# Sources

Free or mostly-free sources. Prefer primary sources (the company that measured the data) over re-posts. Always record the month the data covers, not just the publication date.

## Charts and monthly rankings
| Source | Good for | How to search |
|---|---|---|
| Sensor Tower blog | Monthly worldwide top-10 revenue/downloads with commentary | `Sensor Tower top mobile games <Month YYYY>` |
| AppMagic (via mobilegamer.biz, gamedevreports.substack.com, gameindustrylibrary.com) | Top 20 downloads and grossing, notable risers | `mobilegamer.biz <month> download charts` |
| SocialPeta | Top-20 downloads/revenue by store, MoM change | `SocialPeta top games <Month YYYY>` |
| Gamesforum (globalgamesforum.com) | **Trending** (growth) list, which surfaces new mechanics early | `biggest mobile games of <Month YYYY> gamesforum` |
| App Store / Google Play top charts | Ground truth for a specific country right now | Store web pages or the store apps |

## Genre and market reports
| Source | Good for |
|---|---|
| AppMagic genre reports (gameindustrylibrary.com) | Half-year genre revenue and download shifts, subgenre growth |
| Azur Games blog | Hyper-casual / hybrid-casual state of the market, release counts by subgenre |
| PocketGamer.biz, mobilegamer.biz, gamesindustry.biz | News, acquisitions, publisher moves |
| Naavik | Strategic analysis (convergence, business models) |
| GameRefinery / Liftoff / AppsFlyer / Adjust reports | Feature trends, UA costs, regional retention |
| InvestGame.net | Regional landscape PDFs (e.g. country reports), M&A |

## Benchmarks (always check the date and genre)
| Source | Typical content |
|---|---|
| Publisher "learn" blogs: Supersonic, Voodoo, Homa, Kwalee, Azur | Prototype KPI targets: CPI, D1, D7, playtime |
| Unity / ironSource / AppLovin resources | Hyper-casual scaling KPIs |
| Tenjin, Appodeal, CAS.AI, Playwire, MAF, AdReact | eCPM by format, country and OS; ad revenue share by OS and country |
| GameAnalytics benchmark reports | Retention and playtime by genre |

## Social trends
| Source | Good for |
|---|---|
| TikTok Creative Center (trending hashtags, sounds, creators) | First-party TikTok trend data by country |
| Weekly trend roundups (agency blogs, trend trackers) | Named formats and sounds with start dates |
| Know Your Meme | Meme origin, timeline, ownership and creator |
| YouTube trending / Shorts, Google Trends | Cross-platform confirmation and search interest over time |
| Reddit (r/gamedev, r/iosgaming, r/AndroidGaming, game subs) | Player reactions and complaints, which point to gaps |

## Store pages of comparable titles
For any mechanic, open the store pages of the top 3–5 comparable games. Read the description, update dates, ratings and the **1–3 star reviews**. Player complaints are the cheapest differentiation research there is.

## Source hygiene
- Prefer data from the last 90 days. Older data is labeled with its date.
- When sources disagree, report the range and name both sources.
- SEO content farms that cite no data source are not evidence.
- Download estimates are model-based. Treat small differences as noise.

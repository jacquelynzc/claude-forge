# Scoring Rubric (7 criteria × 1–5 = 35)

Use the anchors. If you are between two anchors, take the lower one unless you have evidence for the higher.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simplicity** (build effort for *this* creator) | Needs a backend, multiplayer, 3D characters or many systems | 2D/simple 3D, a few systems, a few weeks | One scene, one mechanic, UI or 2D, MVP in ≤ 2 weeks |
| **Content cost** (cost of the 100th level or item) | Every level is hand-designed and hand-drawn | Templates + hand tuning | Procedural generation + an automated solvability/difficulty check |
| **Virality** (organic potential) | Muted gameplay clip is boring or unclear | Clip is readable but ordinary | Muted 3-second clip is satisfying, and play produces a shareable result |
| **Monetization** | No natural ad breaks or purchase reasons | Natural interstitial breaks + one rewarded hook | Several natural rewarded hooks (retry, booster, slot) + an IAP reason (no-ads, packs, meta) |
| **Competition gap** | Big publishers hold the top spots, many clones | Several competitors, clear complaints in reviews | Few direct competitors, or a twist the leaders lack |
| **Trend timing** | Saturated or fading | Peaking | Emerging or rising, with ≥ 3 months of expected window |
| **Creator fit** | Needs skills, money or time the profile doesn't have | Needs one weak skill (fixable with a cheap asset or a tool) | Plays to the profile's strengths (e.g. programming, automation, local insight) |

## Totals
- **≥ 24 and no score of 1:** GO candidate
- **18–23:** PIVOT candidate
- **< 18:** KILL

Hard gates (IP, budget, build time > window, saturated with no visible twist) override totals. See `SKILL.md`.
